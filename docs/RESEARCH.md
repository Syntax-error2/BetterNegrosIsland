# Research use of BetterNegrosIsland

Start at [Research & methodology](https://betternegrosisland.vercel.app/methodology#research). The page is readable before the welcome acknowledgment. It provides a snapshot citation, a downloadable research-notes file and a field guide for the base FY2027 comparison CSV.

The portal supports descriptive and exploratory reading of published budget documents. It is not a complete census of NIR funding, a peer-reviewed dataset, an expenditure ledger, an audit conclusion or evidence of an official's sponsorship or wrongdoing. Research purpose does not establish an exemption from applicable privacy duties or source restrictions.

## A reproducible workflow

1. Define the question, unit of analysis, source programs, fiscal years and budget stages. Record all query terms, filters and exclusions.
2. Download the filtered data from the existing explorer/history/reading view and research notes from Methodology. Keep the exact snapshot version, loaded manifest and source notices. The notes contain the displayed manifest rather than a later fetch of a mutable manifest URL.
3. Preserve referenced JSON/PDF files and record access dates where lawful. Verify retained snapshot files against the manifest's SHA-256 checksums. A URL, timestamp or hash alone does not supply permanent archival hosting.
4. Use program plus source record ID for comparison-row identity. Title similarity alone does not establish a match. Keep source uncertainty flags and document sensitivity results with and without uncertain or title-only records.
5. Compare matching stages and readings. Exact PHP amounts are nominal, not inflation-adjusted. Missing is not zero; NEP and House versions are never added. Historical programs and geographic searches have changing coverage and classifications.
6. Verify findings on the original document's page or row. Cite the originating agency, document title, fiscal year, stage/reading, page/row, source URL and access date alongside the portal snapshot citation. Adapt the citation to your institution's style.
7. Report uncertainty, missingness, overlaps and coverage limitations with results. Avoid causal, spending, completion, sponsorship or misconduct conclusions unsupported by independent evidence.

## Source and privacy boundaries

The source software's MIT license is retained; it does not itself license every third-party document or asset. Follow the source notices attached to the data. Review titles and PDFs for unrelated personal details before sharing. Do not enter participant records, identifiers, private contact details or sensitive personal information into searches or public issues.

The new tools copy public citation text or download notes locally after a button click. They add no research-participant collection, account, analytics or remote submission service. The existing privacy/corrections contact, rights procedure, provider retention review and Philippine legal-review gaps remain unresolved; see [the publication risk recheck](qa/vercel-deployment/RISK_RECHECK.md).
