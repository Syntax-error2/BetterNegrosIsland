## Required operator/legal decisions before publication

- Supply a verified monitored privacy contact and corrections/rights intake channel. The owner replied with footer/operator wording, without a contact address or reporting channel. Do not invent one or imply the NPC is the portal's request handler.
- Assign responsibility and practical handling for access, rectification, objection, erasure/blocking, portability, complaints and other applicable rights. Specify response/identity-verification procedures proportionate to actual requests.
- Document the applicable lawful processing criteria for delivery/security logs, notice storage and public-source personal information. Do not treat the entry checkbox as consent for every activity.
- Select hosting/CDN and identify processors, processing locations, technical log categories, access controls, retention/deletion, international transfers and incident handling. Verify the real production headers and contracts.
- Review public-source personal information, source restrictions, appropriate minimization and linked PDFs. A zero pattern flag count is not exhaustive clearance.
- Obtain operator/professional approval of the exact Terms and Privacy Notice version and publication decision. No approval is created by this implementation or removal of the footer pending sentence.

Review references: NPC [Data Privacy Act](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/), [IRR](https://privacy.gov.ph/implementing-rules-regulations-data-privacy-act-2012/), and [right to be informed](https://privacy.gov.ph/the-right-to-be-informed/). The last includes disclosure of controller contact, purposes, processing basis, recipients and retention information; missing operator/provider facts remain an explicit release blocker.
