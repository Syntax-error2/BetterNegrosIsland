# Publication risk recheck — 3 November 2026

This is an engineering/content risk review, not a legal opinion, legal approval, exhaustive privacy assessment or guarantee against complaints, civil claims, criminal investigation or imprisonment. No automated tool or disclaimer supplies such a guarantee.

## What was checked

- Current source-code wording on project details, record status chips, methodology, the regional reading page, public legal notices, evidence images, CSV exports and operator information.
- Current verified snapshot `2026-11-02-37334582ca74`: 866 comparison records, 851 source-confirmed NIR records and 5,253 historical families. Region and locality associations remain distinct.
- The latest recursive published-data scan inspected 13 JSON files and 268,957 strings, including nested raw fields. Zero configured pattern matches were found. It does not identify every person's information or clear linked source PDFs.
- The published-data audit exits unsuccessfully when its configured sensitive-data rules match. Scheduled data refresh/publication is not enabled, so the live site is not silently republishing unreviewed future API additions.
- Cookie notice deployment passed 69 tests and Vercel/GitHub checks. The sole application cookie stores a common dismissal version, not a visitor ID; the Privacy Notice describes it. No analytics, ads, accounts or citizen-submission service was added.

## Existing safeguards verified in the owning source

- Amounts retain budget stages and dated source references; proposals are not presented as actual spending or project completion.
- House-only classifications are candidate document comparisons, not proven concealment or wrongdoing. Geographic/office associations do not claim that a named official requested or controlled a project.
- Fourteen source-marked unconfirmed change pairings are excluded from the regional reading list by default; opting in preserves uncertainty labels.
- Evidence images and CSVs carry source/context limitations and responsible-sharing text.
- Operator identity and the absence of government/BetterGov endorsement are disclosed. BetterGov is not represented as the operator's appointed privacy handler.
- The upstream MIT license and source attribution are retained. The code license is not blanket permission for every third-party document, personal detail, logo or other asset.
